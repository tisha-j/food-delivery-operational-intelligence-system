# Food Delivery Profitability & Operational Intelligence System

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Operational Analytics system designed to analyze food delivery operations, identify profitability drivers, monitor operational risks, and generate strategic business insights.

The system combines:

* Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* SQL-based Business Analytics
* KPI Engineering
* Multi-layer Power BI Dashboards
* Strategic Business Recommendations

The goal of this project is to simulate a real-world analytics workflow used by operations teams, business analysts, and executive decision-makers in delivery and logistics companies.

---

# Business Problem

Food delivery platforms face operational challenges such as:

* Traffic-related delays
* Weather disruptions
* Inefficient fleet allocation
* High-risk deliveries
* Profitability fluctuations

This project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* Which operational conditions reduce profitability?
* Which vehicle types are operationally efficient?
* How do traffic and weather impact delivery performance?
* Which deliveries are operationally risky?
* What strategic actions can improve operational margins?
